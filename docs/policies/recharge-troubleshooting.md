# Mobile Recharge Troubleshooting

This document is support guidance for failed, pending, incomplete, wrong-number, and duplicate mobile recharges. Recharge status, payment status, and operator or provider information must come from authoritative backend systems. Support must never assume that a recharge benefit was delivered without confirmation.

## Recharge failed

Customers may report "my recharge failed", "the recharge did not go through", "recharge unsuccessful", "the payment was taken but recharge failed", or "mobile recharge error".

A mobile recharge may fail because of payment failure, temporary operator issues, an invalid mobile number, provider communication errors, or temporary network problems.

Support steps:

1. Identify the recharge record.
2. Verify customer ownership.
3. Retrieve the latest recharge status from the authoritative system.
4. Retrieve provider information when available.
5. Explain the verified result to the customer.
6. Escalate unresolved issues according to the support workflow.

Do not describe a failed recharge as successful, and do not promise a refund or reversal unless the payment or refund system confirms that outcome.

## Recharge pending

Customers may report "my recharge is pending", "the recharge is stuck", "the operator has not confirmed it", or "the recharge shows processing".

A pending recharge is still awaiting a final result. Support steps:

1. Retrieve the latest recharge status.
2. Check the available provider or operator information.
3. Inform the customer of the verified pending state.
4. Avoid declaring the recharge successful or failed until the provider or authoritative system returns a final result.
5. Recheck or escalate according to the configured support workflow if the recharge remains unresolved.

## Payment successful but recharge benefit not received

Customers may report "money was deducted but my recharge was not done", "the payment succeeded but the mobile number is not recharged", "I paid but did not get the recharge", or "the recharge benefit is missing".

This situation requires investigation. Support steps:

1. Verify the payment status.
2. Verify the recharge status.
3. Retrieve the available operator or provider information.
4. Determine whether the recharge was actually completed.
5. Create a complaint when the issue cannot be resolved automatically.

Do not claim that the recharge was completed or that the benefit was delivered unless the authoritative recharge or provider system confirms it.

## Wrong mobile number recharge

Customers may report "I recharged the wrong number", "I entered the wrong mobile number", "the recharge went to another number", or "can you move my recharge".

Support must not promise that a completed recharge can be reversed or moved to another number.

Support steps:

1. Verify the recharge record.
2. Confirm the authoritative recharge status.
3. Explain the available support options.
4. Create a complaint only when supported by the configured workflow.
5. Escalate when the operator or provider requires manual review.

## Duplicate recharge

Customers may report "I was charged twice for one recharge", "duplicate recharge", "two recharges were made", or "my mobile was recharged more than once".

Support should investigate whether multiple recharge transactions were created. Compare:

- Recharge references.
- Amounts.
- Timestamps.
- Mobile number where authorized.
- Provider status.

Create or escalate a complaint for duplicate successful recharge cases when investigation is required. Do not promise an automatic reversal; report only the outcome confirmed by the payment or recharge system.
